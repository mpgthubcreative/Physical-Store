Email Mode

Controls whether Buddy sends any email at all, and to whom.

  • OFF — nothing sends. New events are recorded but permanently marked suppressed.
  • QA — every otherwise-sendable email redirects to the configured QA recipient only. Requires EMAIL_QA_OVERRIDE_RECIPIENT to be set.
  • LIVE — sends to real customer/admin addresses. Refused if a QA override is still configured.
  • Test orders (isTest:true) never send real email in any mode.
